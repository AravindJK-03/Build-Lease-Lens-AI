# Decision document

## 1. Scope of version one

Version one will:

- Accept one text-based rental agreement PDF upload.
- Extract the rent, deposit, lock-in period, and notice period.
- Flag up to eight risky clauses, each with an exact quote, a plain-language meaning, and questions to ask the landlord.
- Reject any flag whose quoted text cannot be found in the agreement.
- Display a disclaimer that the analysis is not legal advice.

Version one will exclude scanned PDFs, saved reviews, login, Tamil output, and legal verdicts.
